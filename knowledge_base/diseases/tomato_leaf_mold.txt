Disease: Tomato Leaf Mold

Crop: Tomato

Cause:
Tomato leaf mold is caused by the fungus Passalora fulva.

Symptoms:
- Pale green or yellow spots appear on the upper surface of leaves.
- Olive-green to brown fungal growth may appear on the underside of leaves.
- Leaves may curl and eventually fall.
- Severe infection can reduce plant growth and yield.

Favorable Conditions:
- High humidity
- Poor ventilation
- Wet foliage
- Crowded plants
- Greenhouse environments with inadequate air circulation

Management:
- Improve ventilation.
- Reduce humidity.
- Avoid prolonged leaf wetness.
- Remove infected leaves.
- Remove infected plant debris.
- Provide adequate spacing.

Treatment:
Registered fungicides may be used when necessary according to local recommendations and product labels.

Prevention:
- Use healthy seeds and seedlings.
- Maintain good ventilation.
- Avoid excessive humidity.
- Inspect plants regularly.
- Remove infected plant material.

Disease: Cucumber Powdery Mildew

Crop: Cucumber

Cause:
Powdery mildew is caused by several fungal pathogens, including Podosphaera xanthii and Erysiphe species.

Symptoms:
- White powdery patches appear on leaves.
- Infection may begin on older leaves.
- Leaves can become yellow and dry.
- Severe infection can reduce photosynthesis and yield.

Favorable Conditions:
- Warm conditions
- High humidity
- Poor air circulation
- Dense plant growth

Management:
- Remove severely infected leaves.
- Improve air circulation.
- Avoid excessive plant density.
- Remove infected crop debris.
- Monitor plants regularly.

Treatment:
Registered fungicides may be used according to local agricultural recommendations. Some products are most effective when applied early.

Prevention:
- Use resistant varieties when available.
- Maintain adequate plant spacing.
- Improve ventilation.
- Monitor plants frequently.
- Maintain good field sanitation.

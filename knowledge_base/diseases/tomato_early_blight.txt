Disease: Tomato Early Blight

Crop: Tomato

Cause:
Tomato early blight is commonly caused by Alternaria solani.

Symptoms:
- Small dark spots appear on older leaves.
- Lesions often develop concentric ring patterns.
- Yellowing may occur around infected areas.
- Lower leaves are usually affected first.
- Stem lesions may develop.
- Fruits may develop dark sunken lesions near the stem.

Favorable Conditions:
- Warm temperatures
- High humidity
- Extended leaf wetness
- Poor air circulation
- Infected plant debris

Management:
- Remove heavily infected leaves.
- Remove crop debris after harvest.
- Maintain good spacing between plants.
- Avoid overhead irrigation.
- Rotate crops where practical.
- Use healthy seedlings.

Treatment:
Registered fungicides may be used to manage early blight when necessary. Follow the product label and local agricultural recommendations.

Prevention:
- Use resistant varieties when available.
- Maintain field sanitation.
- Avoid working with wet plants.
- Water at the soil level.
- Monitor lower leaves regularly.

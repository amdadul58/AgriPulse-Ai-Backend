Disease: Potato Late Blight

Crop: Potato

Cause:
Potato late blight is caused by Phytophthora infestans.

Symptoms:
- Water-soaked green or dark lesions appear on leaves.
- Lesions become brown or black.
- White growth may appear under humid conditions.
- Stems can become infected.
- Tubers may develop brown or reddish-brown internal lesions.
- Disease can spread rapidly during wet weather.

Favorable Conditions:
- Cool and wet weather
- High humidity
- Frequent rainfall
- Long periods of leaf wetness

Management:
- Remove severely infected plant material.
- Destroy infected crop debris.
- Improve field drainage.
- Avoid unnecessary overhead irrigation.
- Monitor crops frequently.

Treatment:
Registered fungicides can be used preventively or according to disease management recommendations. Always follow product labels and local regulations.

Prevention:
- Use certified disease-free seed potatoes.
- Select resistant varieties where available.
- Maintain good field sanitation.
- Monitor weather and disease conditions.
- Remove volunteer potato plants.
